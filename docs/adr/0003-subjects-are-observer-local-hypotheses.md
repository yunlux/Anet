# Subjects are observer-local hypotheses

Anet models cryptographically verifiable action sources as Actors and models
the latent human, AI, team, or hybrid entity behind them only as an
observer-local Subject hypothesis. Relationship circles attach to those
revisable hypotheses, while trust and authorization continue to attach to
verified Actors and explicit grants; this preserves useful social inference
without turning uncertain entity resolution into a global identity or security
authority.
