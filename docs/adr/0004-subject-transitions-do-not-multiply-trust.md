# Subject transitions do not multiply trust

Splitting, merging, or superseding a Subject hypothesis creates new local
hypotheses and preserves the old hypotheses as superseded history. A
one-to-one supersession may inherit its source relationship, while split and
merge replacements default to `known` unless the operator explicitly selects
exactly one source-to-replacement inheritance path; this avoids copying one
social relationship or contextual trust estimate onto several uncertain
entities while keeping an auditable lineage.
